A studio, not a command prompt
From a few words
to a moving world.
Start with a thought. Shape the motion. Keep the scene. Nothing more to learn before you begin.

        

          
01
DESCRIBE
Give it a subject.
A flower opening, a ring in orbit, a quiet house after dark. Begin in plain language.

          
02
COMPOSE
Let the scene take shape.
Use the built-in offline composer, or connect an AI model you choose. Your prompt becomes a safe scene plan.

          
03
MAKE IT YOURS
Play. Refine. Keep.
Adjust detail and timing, revisit local history, and export JSON or a standalone animation.
